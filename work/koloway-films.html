Six pages for a film studio, built around one rule: oxblood carries the interface, and every other colour is left to the films.

The brief

A website for a film studio: a showreel, the films, a page of stills, services, the studio itself and a way to get in touch. The studio's own copy and footage were still to come, so the design had to work before the content existed. Every word on the current build is placeholder text, and the layout is built to take real films without changing.

One colour for the interface

Film sites tend to go black and let the work speak. This one goes dark too, but gives the interface one colour of its own: oxblood. It carries the header band, the navigation and the active states. Everything else is cream type on a deep green-black, and the only other colour, a pale sage, is kept for the pictures.

Oxblood carries the interface. Mint survives only inside the pictures.

From two comments in the stylesheet

Type that defers to the logo

The studio's wordmark is hand-drawn, with high-contrast strokes and long swashes, and it leads the home page. Yeseva One picks up that contrast for headings without imitating the logo. Jost at weight 300 keeps body text light and quiet. DM Mono is reserved for the technical captions a film person reads: aspect ratio, runtime, format.

Frames, not cards

Work is shown in frames with real film proportions, 2.39:1 for the showreel and 16:9 for the rest, instead of a generic card shape. A fine grain lies over the whole site. It is an SVG noise filter written into the stylesheet, so it costs no image download.
